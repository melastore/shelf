Two new ways in, a theme setting, and fixes for hiding.

- Knock code unlock: four unmarked quarters, tapped in order.
- Optional fake crash: the entry gesture shows an app-has-stopped dialog, and
  a long press on the message carries on to the unlock screen.
- Theme: follow system, light, dark, or AMOLED.
- Shelf can be kept out of the recent apps list.
- Folders at the top of internal storage can be hidden by rename again.
- Fixes a root-hidden folder that could not be unhidden.
