Fixes an unhide that froze the app, and makes hiding and unhiding faster.

- Unhiding no longer freezes the app, and no longer shows Android's "Shelf
  isn't responding" dialog. The whole hide and restore ran on the drawing
  thread, so nothing repainted until the folder was done.
- Unhiding a folder used to open and read every file once just to see which
  ones were protected, then open them all again to unprotect them. It only
  reads them once now.
- File names are put back several at a time, the way they were already
  scrambled several at a time.
- Listing a folder takes one question to the storage provider instead of three
  per file. A hide walks the folder more than once, so this is felt twice.
