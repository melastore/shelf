Look inside a hidden folder without unhiding it, and two new ways in.

- Photos and videos in a hidden folder can be browsed in place. Their real
  names come back from the folder's own manifest, and nothing is written to
  disk.
- The calculator disguise can take your PIN on its keypad. Type it, press
  equals, and there is no gesture left for anyone to find.
- Double tapping the app name now opens private space, as an alternative to
  holding it.
- Settings has a guide covering the disguise, the way back in, the second PIN,
  and what to do before uninstalling.

Also fixed: a root-hidden folder is always put back out of sight when the
viewer closes, a folder holding two files of the same name no longer breaks
the grid, and a file is no longer taken for video because the bytes "ftyp"
turn up somewhere inside it.
