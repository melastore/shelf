Unlock with a password or a pattern, not only a PIN.

- Choose a PIN, a password, or a 3×3 pattern at setup, and change it in Settings.
- The unlock screen no longer mentions that a second credential exists.
- Wrong credentials are logged and reported on your next real unlock.
- Each lockout now lasts longer than the last, up to an hour.
- The list in the second space can be edited so it reads as yours.
- The one-tap hide notification comes back after a restart.
- Fixes a refused root request surfacing as an error instead of a refusal.
- Fixes a conflicted folder that could not be hidden or counted correctly.
