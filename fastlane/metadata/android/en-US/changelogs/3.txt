Redesigned interface, and a decoy PIN that does something useful.

- Rebuilt every screen: new colour scheme per decoy, new keypad, new icons.
- The decoy PIN now opens a convincing private space instead of backing out,
  and its use is reported to you on your next real unlock.
- Five taps in the top-right corner always work, whatever gesture is set.
- Settings: unhide everything, including folders whose record was lost.
- The folder picker now opens on your storage instead of an empty Recents list.
- More reliable restores, with messages that say what actually went wrong.
- Optional strong-biometric unlock, with the PIN always available as fallback.
- Private-move folders now use random payload names to be less obvious.
- Removed file locking to concentrate on folder hiding.
