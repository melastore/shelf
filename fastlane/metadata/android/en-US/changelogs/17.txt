Four new ways to open, hide, and look inside the vault.

- Typing your PIN into the calculator disguise now unlocks private space
  directly, so you never have to reveal that the calculator is a front.
- Turning the phone face down locks private space and hides your folders. On
  by default, and switchable in settings.
- The decoy vault can be filled with realistic sample folders, so a duress
  unlock shows something believable instead of an empty vault.
- Photos can be viewed straight from a locked folder. They are decrypted in
  memory only and never written to disk.

Also fixed: auto-hide now runs when you leave the decoy with folders exposed,
folders deleted outside Shelf no longer linger in the list, and going back
during first-run setup works as expected.
