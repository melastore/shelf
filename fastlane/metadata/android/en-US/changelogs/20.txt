Read documents and play audio without unhiding, and a much faster hide.

- PDFs and text files open alongside photos and video, listed one to a row with
  their full names. A PDF is rendered from memory alone and never written out,
  so no other app is ever handed it.
- Audio in a hidden folder plays in place, decrypted as it streams.
- Video turns the screen to suit the clip, and turns it back on the way out.
- An emergency PIN can be set that destroys keys and journals the moment it is
  entered, for when you are made to unlock.
- Everything can be locked and hidden as soon as a USB cable is plugged in.
- Hiding is much faster. A file is opened once for the whole of its lock rather
  than a dozen times, and several files are worked at once.
- Locked files no longer share a fixed trailer length, and the times they were
  last modified survive being locked.

Also fixed: hiding no longer fails on Shelf's own recovery marker, names are
restored when the manifest was written through a document provider, a folder
record can be forgotten for good instead of coming back on the next reload,
browsing a folder whose grant has gone stale no longer closes the app, and the
calculator no longer offers its keypad to a password or a pattern it could
never take.
