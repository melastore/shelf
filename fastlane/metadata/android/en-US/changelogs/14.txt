Fixes for date formats, folder walks, and the corner tap.

- The calendar and habit disguises now write dates the way your language and
  region do, instead of always putting the day before the month.
- Header and name protection no longer follows a linked folder out of the one
  you are hiding.
- The five-tap corner gesture is no longer thrown off by the clock changing.
- Your credential is cleared from memory even when unlocking fails partway.
